A road is better with company.

Etim Ubong Charles Run Club is a global community of runners. We meet on streets, tracks and trails in cities around the world, at every pace, and nobody runs alone unless they want to.

Every pace belongs

Walk-run, tempo or marathon training. We keep groups by pace and always wait at the corner.

Show up, then go far

Consistency beats speed. Our weekly runs make the habit easy, and the people make it stick.

One club, many cities
